Privacy Policy for SmartHR

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to SmartHR. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the SmartHR mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

As an employee management and HR administration tool, SmartHR collects specific data required for employment purposes:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and employee ID.
  • Location Data: Precise and approximate location data. This is only collected when you use the Geofenced Attendance feature to verify you are at an approved work location.
  • Camera and Photos: The app accesses your camera to capture facial data for "Face Liveness Detection" during attendance punch-ins, ensuring secure authentication. We also collect photos you voluntarily upload for your profile or as document proofs (e.g., medical certificates for leaves).
  • Files and Documents: Documents uploaded by you for official HR purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is strictly used for:

  • Verifying employee identity and attendance.
  • Processing payroll and leave management.
  • Internal company communication and administration.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of company systems.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party advertisers. Your data is only shared with:

  • Your employer and authorized HR administrators.
  • Trusted cloud service providers (like Firebase) strictly for hosting and processing data securely on behalf of your employer.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption (both in transit and at rest) to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as you are employed or as required by local labor laws. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and associated data. To do so, please contact your organization's HR department or visit our Support & Data Deletion page.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes via the app or email.
