Account & Data Deletion Request

SmartHR is an enterprise application. Your account is tied to your employment with your organization. To request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data (including location history, face data, and profile details), you must formally request this through your HR department.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Contact your company's designated HR Administrator.
  2. Submit a formal request for Account Deletion / Data Removal.
  3. Once your HR Administrator processes your offboarding or deletion request on the Admin Portal, all your personal data will be securely purged from our active databases within 30 days.

Note: Certain payroll and attendance records may be retained longer strictly for legal and tax compliance purposes as mandated by local laws.

If you are an HR Administrator and need technical assistance to delete an employee's data, please contact support@shrote.com.
